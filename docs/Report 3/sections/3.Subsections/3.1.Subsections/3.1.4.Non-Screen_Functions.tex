&
The Dispute Management feature provides formal arbitration workflows for conflicts arising on funded milestones. Contract participants can open a formal dispute (\texttt{WAITING\_RESPONSE}) with claims and evidence files, freezing milestone escrow funds. Users can list their disputes, view detailed case files, and inspect contextual dispute statuses directly on milestones. The designated respondent can accept or decline the proposal, or the initiator can withdraw the dispute (\texttt{CANCELLED}) before escalation. Declined or overdue cases can be escalated by a participant to \texttt{UNDER\_REVIEW}. Dedicated domain expert arbitrators (\texttt{EXPERT} role) review escalated disputes via \texttt{/api/expert/disputes} and issue a binding settlement decision (\texttt{FINALIZED}) that distributes the escrow balance.
