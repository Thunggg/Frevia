\textbf{Automated Email \& OTP Dispatch} &
\texttt{EmailService.sendOTP()} \newline
Trigger: Registration and password reset events (SMTP via Nodemailer) &
Dispatches transactional verification and password-reset emails carrying 6-digit OTP codes asynchronously via Gmail SMTP without exposing any standalone web page.

2 &
\textbf{Real-Time Messaging \& Notification Gateway} &
\texttt{ConversationsGateway}, \texttt{AppNotificationService}, \texttt{NotificationRealtimeService} \newline
Trigger: Authenticated Socket.IO events \& domain state transitions &
Maintains authenticated per-user Socket.IO rooms to deliver chat messages, typing indicators, read receipts, and real-time domain notifications (proposals, contracts, milestones, escrow payments, disputes, and moderation) immediately upon persistence.
\\ \hline

3 &
\textbf{Scheduled Milestone Timeout \& Escrow Sweep} &
\texttt{PaymentAutomationService.\allowbreak{}runScheduledChecks()} \newline
Trigger: Periodic background timer every 5 minutes (\texttt{AUTOMATION\_SWEEP\_INTERVAL\_MS}) &
Executes a non-overlapping scheduled sweep that (i) auto-approves milestone submissions pending client review past 7 days (\texttt{CLIENT\_REVIEW\_TIMEOUT\_MS}), releases Stripe Connect escrow payouts, and completes exhausted contracts/jobs; and (ii) marks overdue active milestones as \texttt{LATE} while notifying both parties.
\\ \hline

4 &
\textbf{Automated Stripe Webhook Processing \& Reconciliation} &
\texttt{StripeService}, \texttt{PaymentService} \newline
Endpoint: \texttt{[POST] /api/payments/webhook} \newline
Trigger: Asynchronous Stripe webhook callbacks &
Verifies Stripe webhook signatures against the raw request body and idempotently processes \texttt{checkout.session.completed}, \texttt{payment\_intent.*}, and \texttt{account.updated} events to advance contract funding states (\texttt{PENDING\_SIGN}), update milestone escrow balances, record transaction ledger entries, and synchronize Connect Express payout readiness.
\\ \hline

5 &
\textbf{Transactional Embedding Outbox Worker} &
\texttt{EmbeddingOutboxService.\allowbreak{}tick()} \newline
Trigger: Background worker every 30 seconds (\texttt{WORKER\_INTERVAL\_MS = 30s}, max 5 retries) &
Polls \texttt{EmbeddingOutboxEvent} rows created during job posting or CV updates and asynchronously indexes section-aware document vectors into ChromaDB via \texttt{RecommendationIndexService}, recovering stuck records and isolating transactional writes from AI service downtime.
\\ \hline

6 &
\textbf{AI Job \& Freelancer Recommendation Engine} &
\texttt{AiRecommendationService} \newline
Trigger: Internal recommendation computation &
Computes contextual matching scores between job descriptions and freelancer profiles using section-aware BGE-M3 semantic embeddings combined with structured rule scoring (skills, experience, budget, preferences) and generates claim-level matching explanations.
\\ \hline

7 &
\textbf{AI Content Moderation Pipeline} &
\texttt{AIModerationService} \newline
Trigger: Forum post and comment creation or update &
Evaluates user-submitted forum posts and comments server-side against the external \texttt{AI\_MODERATION\_URL} (\texttt{/moderate}) endpoint, classifying submissions as \texttt{APPROVED}, \texttt{PENDING} (routed to the admin queue), or \texttt{REJECTED} (moved to forum trash), defaulting safely to \texttt{APPROVED} if the AI service is unreachable.
\\ \hline

8 &
\textbf{AI Biometric eKYC \& Duplicate Identity Detection} &
\texttt{IdentityAiService} \newline
Trigger: Identity verification submission (\texttt{ai-check} / \texttt{liveness-pose}) &
Extracts citizen identity fields via RapidOCR, computes facial embeddings and matches selfies via UltraFace/ArcFace (ONNX Runtime), validates active liveness poses with MediaPipe, and detects duplicate \texttt{identityNumber} collisions across accounts before administrative review.
\\ \hline

9 &
\textbf{AI CV Skill Extraction Pipeline} &
\texttt{AiCvAnalyzerService} \newline
Trigger: Freelancer CV analysis request &
Parses uploaded PDF curriculum vitae documents on the backend and extracts normalized candidate skill suggestions for draft review before any profile skills are persisted.
\\ \hline

10 &
\textbf{Automated Language Compliance Verification} &
\texttt{FastTextService} \newline
Trigger: Job text validation / startup pre-loaded WASM model &
Strips HTML and noise from submitted job text and executes an in-process FastText WASM language detector combined with Vietnamese diacritic checks to enforce English-language policy compliance automatically.
\\ \hline

11 &
\textbf{Automated Contract PDF Generation \& Media Storage} &
\texttt{ContractPdfService}, \texttt{CloudinaryService} \newline
Trigger: Contract generation / PDF export &
Renders formal multi-party contract agreements (party metadata, terms, milestone schedules, fee breakdowns, and digital signature timestamps) into PDF documents via \texttt{pdf-lib} and persists/cleans up media assets through Cloudinary and protected local storage.
\\ \hline
