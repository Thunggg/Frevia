    BR-JOB-01 & Public Open Visibility: Public job listings display strictly non-deleted jobs in \texttt{OPEN} status belonging to active, non-banned clients; \texttt{DRAFT}, \texttt{CLOSED}, \texttt{IN\_PROGRESS}, \texttt{COMPLETED}, \texttt{CANCELLED}, and soft-deleted jobs are excluded. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-02 & Multi-Criteria Overlapping Filter and Search: Text search matches case-insensitively across title, description, and skill names; budget bounds match overlapping offered budget ranges; skill filters support single or multi-skill matching; all active filters combine using logical AND. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-03 & Active Category Filter Visibility: Category filtering matches strictly non-deleted categories in \texttt{ACTIVE} status; inactive or soft-deleted categories return no public matches for that filter. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-04 & Pagination and Default Ordering: Public job browsing enforces a positive page number and 1--20 items per page (default 10), sorted newest-first by creation timestamp unless overridden by supported sort options. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-05 & Public vs. Owner Visibility Boundary: Public job detail access exposes strictly non-deleted \texttt{OPEN} jobs of active, non-banned clients; non-owners cannot inspect \texttt{DRAFT}, \texttt{CLOSED}, \texttt{IN\_PROGRESS}, \texttt{COMPLETED}, \texttt{CANCELLED}, or soft-deleted jobs. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-06 & Client Owner Job Detail Isolation: Client management detail views restrict access to the \texttt{CLIENT} role and strictly isolate non-deleted job inspection to the owning client across all lifecycle statuses. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-07 & Complete Metadata, Quota, and Apply Eligibility: Job details expose fixed-price budget bounds, distinct proposal \texttt{expiryDate} vs. project \texttt{deadline}, hiring quota (\texttt{SINGLE} or \texttt{MULTIPLE} with filled vs. required slots), skills, and category tags; applying is permitted only while \texttt{OPEN}, unexpired, and under quota. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-08 & Client Role and Ownership Isolation: Client job management listings enforce the \texttt{CLIENT} role and filter strictly to non-deleted jobs owned by the authenticated client. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-09 & Lifecycle Status and Proposal Aggregate Visibility: Clients can inspect and filter their own jobs across all lifecycle statuses (\texttt{DRAFT}, \texttt{OPEN}, \texttt{IN\_PROGRESS}, \texttt{CLOSED}, \texttt{COMPLETED}, \texttt{CANCELLED}) alongside live total proposal counts. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-10 & Lazy Auto-Close of Expired Open Jobs: Whenever a Client's job list is retrieved, any non-deleted \texttt{OPEN} job whose \texttt{expiryDate} has passed automatically closes to \texttt{CLOSED}, while its pending \texttt{SUBMITTED} proposals transition atomically to \texttt{EXPIRED}. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-11 & Client Creation and Draft Initialization: Only authenticated \texttt{CLIENT} accounts may create jobs; every newly created job initializes in \texttt{DRAFT} status with a generated unique URL slug, and callers cannot assign arbitrary initial statuses. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-12 & English Language and Duplicate Title Enforcement: Job title (10--255 characters) and description (20--5000 characters) must pass English language detection; pre-validation checks also warn against case-insensitive duplicate job titles owned by the same client. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-13 & Budget, Timeline, and Skill Bounds: Fixed-price jobs require minimum and maximum budgets of at least \$10 with \texttt{budgetMin} $\le$ \texttt{budgetMax}, valid future timelines where current time $<$ \texttt{expiryDate} $<$ \texttt{deadline}, and 1--10 valid skills. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-14 & Single Active Category Assignment: Every job must link to exactly one non-deleted \texttt{ACTIVE} job category; selecting multiple categories or an inactive category is prohibited. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-15 & Hiring Quota and Sync Side Effects: \texttt{SINGLE} hiring fixes required positions at 1 and \texttt{MULTIPLE} allows 2--100 positions with filled positions initialized at 0; job creation invalidates category caches and enqueues an embedding index update. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-16 & Draft Completeness Gate on Publish: Only the owning \texttt{CLIENT} may publish a job currently in \texttt{DRAFT} status, after re-verifying English language compliance, field lengths, budgets $\ge$ \$10, future \texttt{expiryDate} before \texttt{deadline}, 1--10 skills, 1 active category, and hiring quota. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-17 & Open Transition, Slug Freeze, and Indexing: Successful publishing transitions status strictly from \texttt{DRAFT} to \texttt{OPEN}, locks the public URL slug, invalidates job category caches, and dispatches an embedding synchronization event. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-18 & Editable Lifecycle States and Ownership: Only the owning \texttt{CLIENT} may edit jobs currently in \texttt{DRAFT}, \texttt{OPEN}, or \texttt{CLOSED} status; jobs in \texttt{IN\_PROGRESS}, \texttt{COMPLETED}, or \texttt{CANCELLED} status prohibit direct edits. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-19 & Slug Immutability and Position Quota Floor: Job URL slugs regenerate on title updates only while in \texttt{DRAFT} status and remain permanent once published; required positions cannot be lowered below already filled positions. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-20 & Existing Proposal Snapshot Preservation: Editing an \texttt{OPEN} job updates only job terms and never alters the submitted \texttt{bidAmount}, \texttt{deliveryDays}, or \texttt{coverLetter} snapshots of existing proposals; freelancers must withdraw and resubmit if wishing to revise terms. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-21 & Transactional Language, Category, and Skill Re-Validation: Updates re-verify English language detection and active category validity, replace skill and category links transactionally, invalidate category caches, and enqueue an embedding update. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-22 & Ownership and Active Contract Guard on Delete: Only the owning \texttt{CLIENT} may delete a job; deletion is prohibited if the job is in \texttt{IN\_PROGRESS} status or linked to any contract in \texttt{PENDING\_SIGN}, \texttt{ACTIVE}, or \texttt{DISPUTED} status. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-23 & Transactional Soft Delete and Proposal Expiration: Deletion sets a soft-delete timestamp to preserve audit integrity and atomically transitions pending \texttt{SUBMITTED} proposals on \texttt{OPEN} jobs to \texttt{EXPIRED}, while clearing caches and removing search index entries. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-24 & Open-State and No-Contract Close Guard: Only the owning \texttt{CLIENT} may close an \texttt{OPEN} job, and closing is prohibited whenever any linked contract exists in \texttt{PENDING\_SIGN}, \texttt{ACTIVE}, or \texttt{DISPUTED} status. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-25 & Closed State and Visibility Removal: Closing updates job status to \texttt{CLOSED}, excludes the job from public search and category listings, clears category caches, and removes the job from vector search indexing. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-26 & Closed-State Ownership Requirement on Reopen: Only the owning \texttt{CLIENT} may reopen a job currently in \texttt{CLOSED} status; jobs in \texttt{DRAFT}, \texttt{OPEN}, \texttt{IN\_PROGRESS}, \texttt{COMPLETED}, or \texttt{CANCELLED} status cannot be reopened. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-27 & 60-Day Reopen Window Enforcement: Closed jobs may reopen only within 60 days of their last update timestamp; requests past the 60-day window are rejected. \\ \hline
    BR-JOB-28 & No Revival of Expired Proposals and Index Restoration: Reopening restores job status to \texttt{OPEN} and re-indexes it for discovery, without reviving previously \texttt{EXPIRED} proposals; freelancers must submit a new proposal if eligible. \\ \hline
    BR-BMK-01 & Freelancer Ownership and Soft-Delete Exclusion: Saved bookmark access strictly enforces the \texttt{FREELANCER} role, scopes results to the caller's user identity, and automatically excludes soft-deleted jobs or banned/deleted clients. \\ \hline
    BR-BMK-02 & Bookmark Preservation Across Job Lifecycle Changes: When a bookmarked job transitions to \texttt{CLOSED}, \texttt{IN\_PROGRESS}, \texttt{COMPLETED}, \texttt{CANCELLED}, or passes its \texttt{expiryDate}, the system preserves the bookmark for tracking rather than silently deleting it, while disabling applications for unavailable jobs. \\ \hline
    BR-BMK-03 & Bookmark Search, Status Filtering, Sorting, and Pagination: Bookmarked job listings support filtering by job status, case-insensitive keyword search on title and description, ascending or descending sorting, and paginated retrieval. \\ \hline
    BR-BMK-04 & Freelancer-Only and Self-Bookmark Guard: Only active \texttt{FREELANCER} accounts may bookmark jobs, and bookmarking one's own posted job is strictly prohibited. \\ \hline
    BR-BMK-05 & Open and Non-Expired Target Check: Bookmarking requires the target job to exist as non-deleted under an active client, hold \texttt{OPEN} status, and have either no \texttt{expiryDate} or a future \texttt{expiryDate}. \\ \hline
    BR-BMK-06 & Composite Uniqueness and Concurrency Protection: Composite uniqueness on \texttt{(userId, jobId)} permits at most one bookmark per freelancer per job; duplicate or concurrent duplicate requests are safely rejected without creating duplicate records. \\ \hline
    BR-BMK-07 & Unconditional Hard Deletion and Ownership Verification: Removing a bookmark verifies target job and existing bookmark ownership before hard-deleting the \texttt{(userId, jobId)} record unconditionally, regardless of whether the job is \texttt{OPEN}, \texttt{CLOSED}, \texttt{COMPLETED}, or expired. \\ \hline
    BR-SS-01 & Authenticated Owner Isolation: Saved search queries and management operations are strictly scoped to the authenticated user's own records so callers cannot view or alter another user's saved searches. \\ \hline
    BR-SS-02 & Name Search and Chronological Ordering: Saved search listings support case-insensitive substring matching on the configuration name and ascending or descending chronological sorting (defaulting to newest first). \\ \hline
    BR-SS-03 & Detail Ownership and Cross-User Isolation: Retrieving a saved search requires matching both the record identifier and the owning user; non-existent or third-party saved searches are treated as not found. \\ \hline
    BR-SS-04 & Parameter Replay Compatibility: Stored search filter parameters preserve structured query criteria for direct replay onto job search while still honoring all public open-job visibility rules. \\ \hline
    BR-SS-05 & Strict Label and Schema Validation: Creating a saved search requires a trimmed label between 1 and 100 characters and valid structured search parameters while rejecting unrecognized fields. \\ \hline
    BR-SS-06 & Maximum Saved Search Quota: Each user may store at most \texttt{MAX\_SAVED\_SEARCHES\_PER\_USER} (30) saved searches; attempts to create additional configurations beyond 30 are rejected. \\ \hline
    BR-SS-07 & Hard Delete with Owner Verification: Deleting a saved search verifies both record ownership and existence before permanently hard-deleting the record; missing or non-owned records cannot be deleted. \\ \hline
    BR-SS-08 & Independent Search Persistence: Removing a saved search deletes only that saved preset and never alters job postings, bookmarks, or job alert subscriptions. \\ \hline
    BR-JA-01 & Freelancer Role Restriction: Only authenticated users holding the \texttt{FREELANCER} role may list, inspect, create, update, delete, or preview job alerts; other roles are denied access. \\ \hline
    BR-JA-02 & User Ownership Isolation and Chronological Ordering: Job alert listings return exclusively alerts owned by the authenticated freelancer, ordered chronologically newest first. \\ \hline
    BR-JA-03 & Detail Ownership and Matching Preview Eligibility: Viewing alert details or matching previews enforces \texttt{FREELANCER} role and record ownership; previews return up to 5 newest non-deleted, unexpired \texttt{OPEN} jobs from active clients matching the alert criteria, excluding self-posted jobs and jobs already proposed to by the freelancer. \\ \hline
    BR-JA-04 & Criteria, Frequency, and Channel Validation: Job alerts require at least one non-empty keyword (up to 200 characters) or at least one skill (up to 20 items), a supported frequency (\texttt{INSTANT}, \texttt{DAILY}, or \texttt{WEEKLY}), and at least one delivery channel (\texttt{IN\_APP} or \texttt{EMAIL}). \\ \hline
    BR-JA-05 & Maximum Alert Quota: Each freelancer may maintain at most \texttt{MAX\_JOB\_ALERTS\_PER\_USER} (10) job alerts; exceeding 10 alerts is rejected. \\ \hline
    BR-JA-06 & Case-Insensitive Duplicate Criteria Prevention: Alert keywords (trimmed lowercase) and skill sets (sorted lowercase) are normalized per user to block duplicate alert configurations upon creation or update. \\ \hline
    BR-JA-07 & Partial Update Ownership and Criteria Integrity: Updating an alert verifies record ownership, ensures at least one keyword or skill criterion remains configured, and rejects updates that duplicate another existing alert belonging to the same user. \\ \hline
    BR-JA-08 & Active Flag Persistence: Toggling an alert's active state (\texttt{isActive}) persists the paused/active status flag while preserving its criteria and quota slot for matching-job queries. \\ \hline
    BR-JA-09 & Hard Delete and Quota Release: Deleting a job alert permanently removes the record and immediately frees one slot within the freelancer's 10-alert limit. \\ \hline
    BR-JA-10 & Non-Owner Deletion Isolation: Attempting to delete an absent alert or an alert owned by another account is rejected as not found without exposing foreign alert existence. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-01 & Freelancer Role and Owned Proposal Scope: Only authenticated \texttt{FREELANCER} accounts may list their own submitted and draft proposals, returning strictly non-deleted records owned by the caller with pagination up to 50 items per page. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-02 & Lazy Proposal Expiration Synchronization: Prior to listing or reviewing proposals, overdue \texttt{SUBMITTED} proposals past \texttt{PROPOSAL\_EXPIRY\_DAYS} (30 days) automatically transition to \texttt{EXPIRED}. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-03 & Freelancer Proposal Detail Ownership: Viewing a freelancer proposal detail requires the \texttt{FREELANCER} role, synchronizes overdue expirations, and restricts access strictly to non-deleted proposals owned by the caller. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-04 & Embedded Job and Client Context Exposure: Proposal detail responses embed the linked job's budget terms, hiring quota, and client summary alongside the freelancer's submitted terms. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-05 & Account Eligibility and Self-Proposal Guard: Submitting or drafting a proposal requires an active, unbanned, non-deleted \texttt{FREELANCER} account; freelancers are forbidden from submitting proposals on their own jobs. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-06 & Job Eligibility and Hiring Slots Gate: Proposals may be created or submitted only for non-deleted \texttt{OPEN} jobs owned by active clients, prior to both \texttt{expiryDate} and \texttt{deadline}, and while open hiring positions remain unfilled. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-07 & Active Uniqueness and Resubmission Cap: At most 1 non-terminal active proposal (\texttt{DRAFT}, \texttt{SUBMITTED}, or \texttt{HIRED}) per job is allowed, and at most \texttt{MAX\_PROPOSALS\_PER\_JOB\_PER\_FREELANCER} (3) total non-deleted proposals per job per freelancer. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-08 & Bid Safeguard and Delivery Timeline Compliance: Bid amounts must fall within \$5 to \$1T USD and within ten times the job's minimum/maximum budget range; cover letters cannot exceed 5,000 characters; delivery days (1--365) must finish before the job deadline. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-09 & Draft Persistence and 30-Day Submission Window: Freelancers may save partial proposals as \texttt{DRAFT} or submit complete proposals as \texttt{SUBMITTED} with a 30-day review window (\texttt{PROPOSAL\_EXPIRY\_DAYS} = 30). \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-10 & Editable Status Window and Ownership: Only the owning \texttt{FREELANCER} may edit proposals in \texttt{DRAFT} or \texttt{SUBMITTED} status; proposals already \texttt{HIRED}, \texttt{REJECTED}, \texttt{WITHDRAWN}, or \texttt{EXPIRED} cannot be modified. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-11 & Submission Completeness and Safeguard Re-Verification: Updating a proposal or submitting a draft requires non-empty cover letter, valid bid amount, and valid delivery days, and re-verifies live job eligibility, bid safeguards, and deadline compliance. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-12 & Owner and Actionable Status Guard on Withdrawal: Only the owning \texttt{FREELANCER} may withdraw a proposal, and withdrawal is permitted solely while it remains in actionable \texttt{SUBMITTED} status after overdue expiration synchronization. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-13 & Permanent Withdrawal Transition: Withdrawing sets proposal status permanently to \texttt{WITHDRAWN} and records \texttt{withdrawnAt}; proposals in \texttt{DRAFT}, \texttt{HIRED}, \texttt{REJECTED}, \texttt{WITHDRAWN}, or \texttt{EXPIRED} status cannot be withdrawn. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-14 & Client Ownership and Pre-Hiring Verification: Only the \texttt{CLIENT} owning a non-deleted \texttt{OPEN} job may hire an actionable \texttt{SUBMITTED} proposal with a valid bid amount while hiring positions remain unfilled. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-15 & Atomic Contract, Fee, Milestone, and Quota Transaction: Acceptance executes inside a single database transaction setting proposal status to \texttt{HIRED}, incrementing filled positions, creating the linked \texttt{Contract} (including a 3\% client platform fee), initializing Milestone 1 as \texttt{PENDING}, and notifying the hired freelancer. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-16 & Single vs. Multiple Hiring Fulfillment: On \texttt{SINGLE} jobs, hiring one applicant automatically rejects competing \texttt{SUBMITTED} proposals and sets the job to \texttt{IN\_PROGRESS}; on \texttt{MULTIPLE} jobs, competing proposals remain \texttt{SUBMITTED} until all required positions are filled. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-17 & Client Ownership and Actionable Status Guard on Rejection: Only the owning \texttt{CLIENT} of a non-deleted job may reject a candidate proposal currently in actionable \texttt{SUBMITTED} status after overdue expiration synchronization. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-18 & Finalized Rejection State: Rejecting sets proposal status to \texttt{REJECTED}, records \texttt{rejectedAt}, and clears \texttt{expiresAt}; proposals already \texttt{DRAFT}, \texttt{HIRED}, \texttt{WITHDRAWN}, \texttt{REJECTED}, or \texttt{EXPIRED} cannot be rejected. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-19 & Client Job Proposal Scope and Cleanup Synchronization: Listing proposals for a job enforces \texttt{CLIENT} job ownership, rejects soft-deleted jobs, excludes \texttt{DRAFT} proposals and banned or deleted applicants, synchronizes overdue expirations, and automatically rejects remaining \texttt{SUBMITTED} proposals if the job was \texttt{CANCELLED}. \\ \hline
    BR-PROP-20 & Client Applicant Detail Authorization and Privacy: Inspecting an individual applicant's proposal requires \texttt{CLIENT} ownership of the non-deleted linked job, excludes drafts, synchronizes overdue expirations, and restricts candidate cover letters, terms, and profile details exclusively to the owning client. \\ \hline
    BR-REC-01 & Role-Specific Recommendation Direction: Job recommendations (\texttt{cv2jd}) are strictly restricted to active \texttt{FREELANCER} callers with an existing freelancer profile, while candidate recommendations (\texttt{jd2cv}) are strictly restricted to the owning \texttt{CLIENT} of the target job. \\ \hline
    BR-REC-02 & Candidate Pool Pre-Filtering and Skill Prioritization: Up to 35 candidates are pre-filtered per request, prioritizing skill overlap; recommended jobs include only non-deleted, unexpired, unfilled \texttt{OPEN} jobs from active non-banned clients (excluding self-posted jobs), and recommended candidates include only active non-banned freelancer profiles with skills (excluding the caller). \\ \hline
    BR-REC-03 & Hybrid Multi-Signal Scoring: Match candidates are ranked using hybrid weighted scoring (\texttt{hybrid-1.0.0}), producing an overall composite score alongside individual semantic, skill, experience, budget, preference, and optional keyword signal components. \\ \hline
    BR-REC-04 & Graceful Fallback on AI Service Degradation: When the AI ranking engine fails or times out, recommendation views fall back to returning eligible unranked items with zeroed scores instead of failing page load, while explanation queries return an unready fallback state. \\ \hline
    BR-REC-05 & Recommendation Traceability and Index Synchronization: Ranked recommendation records are persisted asynchronously so explanation requests can verify requester ownership against historical match factors, while outbox and reindexing workflows keep vector embeddings synchronized. \\ \hline
    BR-FCAT-01 & Public Access and Active Category Visibility: Job category directory and detail views are publicly accessible without authentication and expose strictly non-deleted categories in \texttt{ACTIVE} status. \\ \hline
    BR-FCAT-02 & Accurate Open Job Count Aggregation: Public open job counts displayed per category tally strictly linked jobs in \texttt{OPEN} status that are non-deleted and belong to active, non-banned clients. \\ \hline
    BR-FCAT-03 & Alphabetical Ordering, Search, and Cache Invalidation: Category listings are ordered alphabetically by name ascending, support case-insensitive search across name and description, and are cached for 300 seconds with automatic invalidation upon client job mutations. \\ \hline
    BR-FCAT-04 & Category Detail Visibility and Open Jobs Feed: Inactive (\texttt{INACTIVE}) or soft-deleted categories cannot be accessed publicly; valid category details paginate strictly \texttt{OPEN}, non-deleted jobs from non-banned active clients with a positive page number and 1--50 items per page. \\ \hline
