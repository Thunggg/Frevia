\subsection{Dispute Management}

The Dispute Management module provides a formal conflict resolution framework for contracts experiencing scope deadlock, substandard deliverables, or unresponsiveness. Filing a claim immediately freezes milestone escrow funds from release or refund. Counterparties can submit formal rebuttals and resolution proposals, or escalate unresolved disputes to platform domain expert arbitrators who formulate binding settlement rulings.
