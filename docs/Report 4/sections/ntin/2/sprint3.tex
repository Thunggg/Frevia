% ================= Dispute Management =================
\subsection{Dispute Management}

Dispute Management covers the UC-33 formal arbitration workflow for funded
milestones: opening a formal dispute case when conflicts arise, listing disputes
with status and deadline badges, viewing comprehensive dispute dossiers with
claims, evidence, and fee status, submitting counter-statements with rebuttal
evidence, responding to arbitrator resolutions, and enabling administrators to
evaluate evidence and propose binding resolution split decisions.
