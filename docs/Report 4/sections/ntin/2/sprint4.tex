% ================= Payment Management =================
\subsection{Payment Management}

Payment Management covers the UC-43 financial transaction and payment
infrastructure integrated with Stripe: connecting freelancer payout bank
accounts via Stripe Connect Express, inspecting payout transfer histories and
breakdowns, funding milestone escrow deposits, issuing milestone refunds,
processing contract platform fees, authorizing milestone releases to
freelancers, paying dispute arbitration fees, inspecting comprehensive account
payment history and receipts, executing multi-party dispute settlement
payouts, processing asynchronous Stripe webhook events, and synchronizing
payment intent statuses with the payment gateway.
