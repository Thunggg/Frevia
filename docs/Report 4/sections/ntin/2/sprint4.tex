\subsection{Payment Management}

The Payment Management module integrates Stripe to support escrow protection, customer payment tokenization, pre-authorized contract hold deposits, and automated freelancer disbursements. It enables freelancers to link verified bank accounts via Stripe Connect Express, clients to manage payment methods, milestone escrow funding via hosted Checkout or saved cards, automated escrow payout release upon deliverable acceptance, and payment transaction history tracking.
