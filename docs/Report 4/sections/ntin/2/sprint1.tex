% ================= Contract Management =================
\subsection{Contract Management}

Contract Management covers the UC-13 contract lifecycle: creating and updating
fixed-price contracts upon proposal acceptance, handling dual-signing between
client and freelancer, marking contracts as completed or cancelled, and
managing contract shared files including viewing, uploading, and deleting
contract attachments.
