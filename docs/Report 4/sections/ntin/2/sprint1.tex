\subsection{Contract Management}

The Contract Management module governs the legal and operational agreement between clients and freelancers. It encompasses the complete lifecycle of fixed-price and milestone-based contracts, from initial offer generation, scope negotiation, and bilateral digital signature, through PDF contract export, file attachment sharing, and contract lifecycle termination (completion, cancellation, or rejection).
