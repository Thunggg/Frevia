\subsection{Milestone Management}

The Milestone Management feature breaks contract scopes into structured, payable deliverables. Clients and freelancers define milestone titles, descriptions, amounts, and expected deadlines, and freelancers sequentially advance funded milestones to \texttt{IN\_PROGRESS}.

\subsection{Milestone Submission Management}

Milestone Submission Management supports deliverable verification and handoff workflows. Freelancers submit completed deliverables along with external proof and repository links; clients review submissions, request modifications with actionable feedback, or approve milestones to initiate escrow release.
