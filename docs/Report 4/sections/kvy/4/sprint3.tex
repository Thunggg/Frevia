\subsection{ForumAdminController Class}

\paragraph{ClassMethod}
\begin{ClassMethodTable}
01 & \code{ForumAdminController(}\newline\code{adminService: ForumAdminService)} &
A method to initialize the controller with the administration service.
\newline -- \code{adminService}: \code{ForumAdminService} -- service dependency.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{void} -- constructor, no return value. \\ \hline

02 & \code{getAdminStats(roleName: string)} &
A method to return the forum dashboard counters.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name from \code{@UserActive('roleName')}.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumAdminStatsType>} -- category, post, comment, report, pending-report, and user totals plus the five newest posts.
\newline -- Route: \code{GET /forums/admin/stats}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

03 & \code{getAdminCategoryLists(}\newline\code{roleName: string, page: number,}\newline\code{limit: number, search: string,}\newline\code{deleted: string,}\newline\code{sortBy: 'id' | 'name' | 'createdAt',}\newline\code{sortOrder: 'asc' | 'desc')} &
A method to list categories with search, deleted-state filtering, and sorting.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name.
\newline -- \code{page}, \code{limit}: \code{number} -- coerced with \code{Number()} and defaulted to 1 and 10.
\newline -- \code{search}: \code{string} -- optional case-insensitive name search.
\newline -- \code{deleted}: \code{string} -- optional \code{'true'} or \code{'false'} filter.
\newline -- \code{sortBy}, \code{sortOrder}: \code{'id' | 'name' | 'createdAt'} and \code{'asc' | 'desc'} -- optional sort controls.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumAdminCategoryListResponseType>} -- categories and pagination.
\newline -- Route: \code{GET /forums/admin/categories}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

04 & \code{getAdminCategoryById(}\newline\code{roleName: string, id: number)} &
A method to return one active category.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name.
\newline -- \code{id}: \code{number} -- category identifier.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumCategoryDetailResponseType>} -- category detail with public post count.
\newline -- Route: \code{GET /forums/admin/categories/:id}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

05 & \code{createAdminCategory(}\newline\code{roleName: string,}\newline\code{body: CreateForumCategoryBodyDto)} &
A method to create a category.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name.
\newline -- \code{body}: \code{CreateForumCategoryBodyDto} -- name and optional description.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumCategoryType>} -- the created category.
\newline -- Route: \code{POST /forums/admin/categories}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

06 & \code{updateAdminCategory(}\newline\code{roleName: string, id: number,}\newline\code{body: UpdateForumCategoryBodyDto)} &
A method to rename or re-describe a category.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name.
\newline -- \code{id}: \code{number} -- category identifier.
\newline -- \code{body}: \code{UpdateForumCategoryBodyDto} -- optional name and optional description.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumCategoryType>} -- the updated category.
\newline -- Route: \code{PATCH /forums/admin/categories/:id}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

07 & \code{deleteAdminCategory(}\newline\code{roleName: string, id: number)} &
A method to soft-delete a category and detach its posts.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name.
\newline -- \code{id}: \code{number} -- category identifier.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<{ message: string }>} -- deletion confirmation.
\newline -- Route: \code{DELETE /forums/admin/categories/:id}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

08 & \code{restoreAdminCategory(}\newline\code{roleName: string, id: number)} &
A method to restore a soft-deleted category.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name.
\newline -- \code{id}: \code{number} -- category identifier.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumCategoryType>} -- the restored category.
\newline -- Route: \code{PATCH /forums/admin/categories/:id/restore}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

09 & \code{getAdminCommentLists(}\newline\code{roleName: string, page: number,}\newline\code{limit: number, search: string)} &
A method to list active comments for administration.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name.
\newline -- \code{page}, \code{limit}: \code{number} -- pagination, defaulted to 1 and 10.
\newline -- \code{search}: \code{string} -- optional case-insensitive content search.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumAdminCommentListResponseType>} -- comments with author and parent-post summaries, plus pagination.
\newline -- Route: \code{GET /forums/admin/comments}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

10 & \code{getPendingPosts(}\newline\code{roleName: string, page: number,}\newline\code{limit: number)} &
A method to list posts waiting for moderation.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name.
\newline -- \code{page}, \code{limit}: \code{number} -- pagination, defaulted to 1 and 10.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<PendingForumPostListResponseType>} -- non-deleted \code{PENDING} posts, oldest first, with pagination.
\newline -- Route: \code{GET /forums/admin/pending-posts}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

11 & \code{getTrashPosts(}\newline\code{roleName: string, page: number,}\newline\code{limit: number)} &
A method to list deleted or rejected posts.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name.
\newline -- \code{page}, \code{limit}: \code{number} -- pagination, defaulted to 1 and 10.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumTrashPostListResponseType>} -- posts with \code{deletedAt} set or moderation status \code{REJECTED}.
\newline -- Route: \code{GET /forums/admin/trash/posts}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

12 & \code{getTrashComments(}\newline\code{roleName: string, page: number,}\newline\code{limit: number)} &
A method to list deleted comments.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name.
\newline -- \code{page}, \code{limit}: \code{number} -- pagination, defaulted to 1 and 10.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumTrashCommentListResponseType>} -- soft-deleted comments with author and parent-post summaries.
\newline -- Route: \code{GET /forums/admin/trash/comments}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

13 & \code{restoreTrashPost(}\newline\code{roleName: string, id: number)} &
A method to restore a post from the trash.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name.
\newline -- \code{id}: \code{number} -- post identifier.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumPostType>} -- the restored post.
\newline -- Route: \code{PATCH /forums/admin/trash/posts/:id/restore}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

14 & \code{restoreTrashComment(}\newline\code{roleName: string, id: number)} &
A method to restore a comment from the trash.
\newline -- \code{roleName}: \code{string} -- current role name.
\newline -- \code{id}: \code{number} -- comment identifier.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumAdminCommentType>} -- the restored comment.
\newline -- Route: \code{PATCH /forums/admin/trash/comments/:id/restore}; administrator endpoint. \\ \hline

\subsection{ForumAdminRepository Class}

\paragraph{ClassMethod}
\begin{ClassMethodTable}
01 & \code{ForumAdminRepository(prisma: PrismaService)} &
A method to initialize the repository with the shared Prisma service.
\newline -- \code{prisma}: \code{PrismaService} -- database client.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{void} -- constructor, no return value. \\ \hline

02 & \code{getPendingPosts(page: number, limit: number)} &
A method to query the moderation queue and its total in one transaction.
\newline -- \code{page}, \code{limit}: \code{number} -- pagination.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<{ posts: PendingForumPostType[]; total: number }>} -- non-deleted \code{PENDING} posts, oldest first, with author summary.

\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumPostType>} -- the reviewed post.

04 & \code{getAdminStats()} &

\newline -- Posts, comments, categories, and users exclude soft-deleted rows; report totals include all rows. \\ \hline

05 & \code{getAdminCommentLists(}\newline\code{page: number, limit: number,}\newline\code{search?: string)} &
A method to query a page of active comments and the matching total in one transaction.
\newline -- \code{page}, \code{limit}: \code{number} -- pagination.
\newline -- \code{search}: \code{string | undefined} -- optional case-insensitive content filter.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<{ comments: ForumAdminCommentType[]; total: number }>} -- comments with author and parent-post summaries, newest first. \\ \hline

A method to query a page of categories and the matching total in one transaction.
\newline -- \code{page}, \code{limit}: \code{number} -- pagination.
\newline -- \code{search}: \code{string | undefined} -- optional case-insensitive name filter.
\newline -- \code{sortBy}, \code{sortOrder}: optional sort controls; defaults are \code{'id'} and \code{'desc'}.
\newline -- \code{deleted}: \code{string | undefined} -- \code{'true'} selects only deleted rows, \code{'false'} only active rows, omitted selects both.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<{ categories: ForumAdminCategoryType[]; total: number }>} -- categories with \code{postCount} and pagination total. \\ \hline

A method to load one active category with its public post count.
\newline -- \code{id}: \code{number} -- category identifier.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumCategoryType | null>} -- the category detail or \code{null}. \\ \hline

A method to create a category with a unique name and generated slug.

\newline -- \code{data.description}: \code{string | null | undefined} -- optional description.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumCategoryType>} -- the created category with \code{postCount = 0}.

\newline -- \code{data}: \code{UpdateForumCategoryBodyType} -- optional name and description.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumCategoryType>} -- the updated category.
\newline -- Throws \code{ForumCategoryNotFoundException} or \code{ForumCategoryAlreadyExistsException}. \\ \hline

10 & \code{deleteAdminCategory(id: number)} &
A method to detach posts and soft-delete a category in one transaction.
\newline -- \code{id}: \code{number} -- category identifier.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<{ message: string }>} -- deletion confirmation.
\newline -- Non-deleted posts in the category are reassigned to \code{categoryId = null} before the category receives \code{deletedAt}. \\ \hline

11 & \code{restoreAdminCategory(id: number)} &
A method to restore a soft-deleted category after a name-conflict check.
\newline -- \code{id}: \code{number} -- category identifier.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumCategoryType>} -- the restored category.
\newline -- Throws \code{ForumCategoryNotFoundException} or \code{ForumCategoryAlreadyExistsException}. \\ \hline

12 & \code{getTrashPosts(page: number, limit: number)} &
A method to query deleted or rejected posts and the matching total in one transaction.
\newline -- \code{page}, \code{limit}: \code{number} -- pagination.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<{ posts: ForumTrashPostType[]; total: number }>} -- trash posts with \code{deletedAt} and author summary.
\newline -- Ordering is by \code{deletedAt} descending with null values last. \\ \hline

13 & \code{getTrashComments(page: number, limit: number)} &
A method to query soft-deleted comments and the matching total in one transaction.
\newline -- \code{page}, \code{limit}: \code{number} -- pagination.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<{ comments: ForumTrashCommentType[]; total: number }>} -- trash comments with \code{deletedAt} and parent-post summary, newest deletion first. \\ \hline

14 & \code{restoreTrashPost(postId: number)} &

\newline -- \code{commentId}: \code{number} -- comment identifier.
\newline -- \textit{Returns:} \code{Promise<ForumAdminCommentType>} -- the restored comment with author and parent-post summaries.
\newline -- Throws \code{ForumCommentNotFoundException} when no deleted row matches. \\ \hline
