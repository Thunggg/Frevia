\section{Representative User Workflows}

Detailed operation guides covering every screen across all 44 functional routes are maintained in Report 6 (Software User Guides). The subsections below present the seven representative end-to-end user journeys of \textbf{Frevia}, spanning onboarding, contract governance, milestone execution, Stripe escrow settlement, dispute arbitration, and AI-assisted community operations.

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Authentication \& Role-Based Administration}
\noindent\textit{Workflow Path: Guest $\rightarrow$ Sign Up (OTP Verification) $\rightarrow$ Login $\rightarrow$ Session Control $\rightarrow$ Admin RBAC Matrix \& Catalogs}

\subsubsection{Purpose}
Allows prospective users to onboard securely with email one-time password (OTP) validation or Google OAuth, manage active device sessions, and enables platform administrators to govern dynamic role permissions and system skill catalogs.

\subsubsection{Step-by-Step User Guide}

\wfstep{Step 1: Register and Verify Account via OTP}
Guests open \texttt{/register}, enter credentials, choose an initial role (\textbf{Client} or \textbf{Freelancer}), and confirm registration with the 6-digit email OTP (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_auth_register}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/auth/m02_01_register.png}{Account registration screen with email OTP verification and role selection}{fig:wf_auth_register}

\wfstep{Step 2: Sign In and Audit Active Device Sessions}
Registered users authenticate at \texttt{/login} (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_auth_login}) and inspect active device sessions with IP/user-agent telemetry to remotely revoke unauthorized logins (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_auth_sessions}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/auth/m02_02_login.png}{Sign-in screen supporting email/password credentials and Google OAuth 2.0}{fig:wf_auth_login}

\wffig[0.85]{images/auth/m02_05_sessions_list.png}{Active device sessions management view}{fig:wf_auth_sessions}

\wfstep{Step 3: Administer RBAC Roles, Permissions \& System Catalogs}
Administrators access \texttt{/admin} (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_admin_dash}), configure roles (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_admin_roles}) and route-level permissions via the RBAC matrix (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_admin_matrix}), and maintain standardized skill tags (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_admin_skills}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/admin/m14_01_admin_dashboard.png}{Administrator operations overview dashboard}{fig:wf_admin_dash}

\wffig[0.85]{images/admin/m14_03_admin_roles.png}{RBAC role management interface}{fig:wf_admin_roles}

\wffig[0.85]{images/admin/m14_04_role_permissions_matrix.png}{Granular route-level RBAC permission assignment matrix}{fig:wf_admin_matrix}

\wffig[0.85]{images/admin/m15_01_admin_skills.png}{Administrator master skills catalog management}{fig:wf_admin_skills}

\subsection{Profile Setup \& eKYC Identity Verification}
\noindent\textit{Workflow Path: Profile Management $\rightarrow$ Upload Identity Card (CCCD) $\rightarrow$ AI Face Liveness Scan $\rightarrow$ Admin Verification Audit}

\subsubsection{Purpose}
Establishes credible participant profiles and verifiable freelancer identities by pairing national identity document upload and AI facial liveness verification with mandatory administrator inspection.

\subsubsection{Step-by-Step User Guide}

\wfstep{Step 1: Manage Client \& Workspace Profiles}
Users maintain biography, company details, and social credentials from their workspace profile panel (Figures~\ref{fig:wf_prof_client} and \ref{fig:wf_prof_workspace}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/client/m3_01_view_client_profile.png}{Account profile overview and presentation settings}{fig:wf_prof_client}

\wffig[0.85]{images/client/m7_01_client_workspace.png}{Client company workspace and saved talent controls}{fig:wf_prof_workspace}

\wfstep{Step 2: Submit eKYC Documents and AI Facial Liveness}
Freelancers upload front and back images of their Vietnamese Citizen Identity Card (CCCD) alongside webcam liveness capture routed to the \texttt{id-verification} AI microservice (Figures~\ref{fig:wf_kyc_upload} and \ref{fig:wf_kyc_face}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/freelancer/m5_01_upload_identity_documents.png}{Identity document (CCCD) upload interface}{fig:wf_kyc_upload}

\wffig[0.85]{images/ai/ai_face_scan_guide.png}{AI facial liveness detection and identity matching workflow}{fig:wf_kyc_face}

\wfstep{Step 3: Audit Verification Requests (Administrator)}
Administrators inspect AI match scores alongside uploaded card scans and approve or reject verified freelancer badges (Figures~\ref{fig:wf_kyc_review} and \ref{fig:wf_kyc_approve}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/admin/identity/m35_02_id_review_guide.png}{Administrator eKYC request inspection queue}{fig:wf_kyc_review}

\subsection{Contract Creation, Bilateral Signing \& Shared Files}
\noindent\textit{Workflow Path: Hire Winning Proposal $\rightarrow$ Create Contract \& Milestones $\rightarrow$ Edit Terms $\rightarrow$ Sign Contract $\rightarrow$ Upload Shared Files $\rightarrow$ Export PDF / Complete}

\subsubsection{Purpose}
Converts accepted proposals into legally binding fixed-price agreements with customizable scope terms, bilateral digital signatures, shared file collaboration, PDF archiving, and formal contract closure.

\subsubsection{Step-by-Step User Guide}

\wfstep{Step 1: Create Contract \& Define Initial Milestones}
When hiring a winning proposal, the Client opens the \textbf{Create Contract \& Hire Freelancer} drawer, sets the total budget and end date, configures milestone splits, and issues the contract (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m13_create}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/contracts/m13_01_create_contract_guide.png}{Create Contract \& Hire Freelancer drawer with milestone builder}{fig:wf_m13_create}

\wfstep{Step 2: Review Agreement Details \& Update Terms}
Prior to bilateral signature completion, the Client can adjust delivery clauses and contract end dates from the agreement workspace (Figures~\ref{fig:wf_m13_update} and \ref{fig:wf_m13_detail}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/contracts/m13_02_update_contract_guide.png}{Edit Contract Agreement drawer for modifying terms and end date}{fig:wf_m13_update}

\wffig[0.85]{images/contracts/m13_04_view_contract_detail_guide.png}{Unified Contract Detail workspace displaying status, milestones, and actions}{fig:wf_m13_detail}

\wfstep{Step 3: Execute Bilateral Digital Signature \& Share Workspace Files}
Once the initial hold fee is confirmed, both Client and Freelancer digitally sign the contract (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m13_sign}) and upload specification assets into the contract's shared files repository (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m13_upload_file}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/contracts/m13_05_sign_contract_guide.png}{Bilateral digital contract signing confirmation modal}{fig:wf_m13_sign}

\wffig[0.85]{images/contracts/m13_10_upload_contract_file_guide.png}{Uploading shared workspace reference files to an active contract}{fig:wf_m13_upload_file}

\wfstep{Step 4: Export PDF Record \& Complete Contract}
Parties can export a formatted PDF copy of the signed contract (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m13_export}), and the Client marks the engagement completed once all milestones conclude (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m13_complete}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/contracts/m13_06_export_contract_pdf_guide.png}{Exporting signed agreement documentation to PDF}{fig:wf_m13_export}

\wffig[0.85]{images/contracts/m13_08_complete_contract_guide.png}{Final contract completion confirmation workflow}{fig:wf_m13_complete}

\subsection{Milestone Deliverable Execution \& Review}
\noindent\textit{Workflow Path: Create Milestone $\rightarrow$ Upload Deliverable Files $\rightarrow$ Submit Deliverable $\rightarrow$ Request Changes / Approve Milestone}

\subsubsection{Purpose}
Breaks active contracts into verifiable staged deliverables, tracking uploaded work assets, formal freelancer submissions, iterative client change requests, and final milestone approval.

\subsubsection{Step-by-Step User Guide}

\wfstep{Step 1: Add Stage Milestone to Active Contract}
The Client defines an additional deliverable stage via the \textbf{Create Milestone} drawer, specifying deliverable expectations, USD allocation, and due date within the contract ceiling (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m24_create}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/milestones/m24_03_create_milestone_guide.png}{Create Milestone drawer for defining staged deliverables}{fig:wf_m24_create}

\wfstep{Step 2: Upload Deliverable Files \& Submit Milestone}
The Freelancer attaches deliverable artifacts (up to 25~MB per file) in the milestone repository (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m25_upload}) and submits the deliverable package with completion notes for client review (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m26_submit}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/milestones/m25_02_upload_milestone_file_guide.png}{Uploading deliverable files into a milestone workspace}{fig:wf_m25_upload}

\wffig[0.85]{images/milestones/m26_01_submit_milestone_guide.png}{Submitting a completed milestone deliverable for client review}{fig:wf_m26_submit}

\wfstep{Step 3: Request Changes or Approve Milestone Release}
The Client inspects submitted artifacts and either issues structured revision feedback with a new deadline (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m26_changes}) or approves the milestone to unlock escrow payout (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m26_approve}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/milestones/m26_04_request_milestone_changes_guide.png}{Client revision request dialog with due date and change notes}{fig:wf_m26_changes}

\wffig[0.85]{images/milestones/m26_05_approve_milestone_guide.png}{Client milestone acceptance confirming deliverable satisfaction}{fig:wf_m26_approve}

\subsection{Stripe Connect Escrow \& Milestone Settlement}
\noindent\textit{Workflow Path: Onboard Stripe Express $\rightarrow$ Pay Contract Hold Fee $\rightarrow$ Fund Milestone Escrow (Checkout / Saved Card) $\rightarrow$ Release Escrow Payout $\rightarrow$ Audit Ledger}

\subsubsection{Purpose}
Provides transparent financial security by holding client funds in Stripe escrow prior to work execution, charging only a flat \$5.00 USD fee per contract, and transferring 100\% of milestone value to the freelancer upon approval.

\subsubsection{Step-by-Step User Guide}

\wfstep{Step 1: Onboard Freelancer Payout Account via Stripe Connect Express}
Before signing contracts, Freelancers link their payout destination using the hosted Stripe Connect Express flow (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m43_connect}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/payments/m43_01_connect_stripe_guide.png}{Stripe Connect Express payout onboarding banner and activation flow}{fig:wf_m43_connect}

\wfstep{Step 2: Authorize Flat \$5.00 Contract Hold Deposit}
Upon contract creation, the Client authorizes the \$5.00 USD platform hold fee to transition the agreement from \texttt{AWAITING\_PAYMENT} into signature readiness (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m43_hold}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/payments/m43_06_contract_hold_deposit_guide.png}{Authorizing the flat \$5.00 USD contract hold fee via Stripe}{fig:wf_m43_hold}

\wfstep{Step 3: Fund Individual Milestone Escrow (Checkout or Saved Card)}
Clients fund upcoming milestones either through hosted Stripe Checkout (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m43_checkout}) or one-click saved card authorization (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m43_savedcard}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/payments/m43_07_fund_milestone_checkout_guide.png}{Funding a milestone into escrow via Stripe Checkout Session}{fig:wf_m43_checkout}

\wffig[0.85]{images/payments/m43_08_fund_milestone_saved_card_guide.png}{Funding a milestone directly with a saved Stripe payment method}{fig:wf_m43_savedcard}

\wfstep{Step 4: Release Escrow Payout \& Inspect Transaction Ledger}
Once the milestone deliverable is approved, the Client releases held escrow funds to trigger a Stripe transfer (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m43_release}), and both parties can audit every settled event in the payment history ledger (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m43_history}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/payments/m43_09_release_milestone_escrow_guide.png}{Releasing funded milestone escrow payout to the freelancer}{fig:wf_m43_release}

\wffig[0.85]{images/payments/m43_10_view_payment_transactions_guide.png}{Reconciled platform payment transactions ledger}{fig:wf_m43_history}

\subsection{Dispute Filing, Arbitration \& Bilateral Reviews}
\noindent\textit{Workflow Path: Open Dispute $\rightarrow$ Submit Respondent Defense $\rightarrow$ Escalate Dispute $\rightarrow$ Expert Arbitrator Decision $\rightarrow$ Post-Contract Review}

\subsubsection{Purpose}
Protects funded milestones against deadlocks through a symmetrical 5-stage arbitration procedure with evidence tracking and split escrow distribution, followed by mutual post-contract reputation reviews.

\subsubsection{Step-by-Step User Guide}

\wfstep{Step 1: Open Milestone Dispute \& Submit Respondent Defense}
Either contracting party initiates a dispute on a funded milestone (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m33_open}), freezing funds until the counterparty files an official response and supporting evidence (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m33_respond}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/disputes/m33_01_open_dispute_guide.png}{Opening a formal dispute with claim statement and evidence}{fig:wf_m33_open}

\wffig[0.85]{images/disputes/m33_05_respond_to_dispute_guide.png}{Submitting the respondent counter-statement and evidence files}{fig:wf_m33_respond}

\wfstep{Step 2: Escalate to Arbitration \& Render Expert Decision}
Unresolved disputes escalate with payment of the arbitration fee (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m33_escalate}), allowing an assigned Expert or Administrator to issue a binding full or split escrow ruling (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_m33_decision}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/disputes/m33_07_escalate_dispute_guide.png}{Escalating an unresolved dispute to formal arbitration review}{fig:wf_m33_escalate}

\wffig[0.85]{images/disputes/m33_10_expert_decision_guide.png}{Expert arbitrator decision form allocating held escrow funds}{fig:wf_m33_decision}

\wfstep{Step 3: Submit Bilateral Contract Reviews \& Public Reply}
Upon completion, Clients and Freelancers publish star ratings and written feedback (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_rev_write}), to which recipients may issue one public response (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_rev_reply}).

\wffig[0.85]{images/community/m11_05_write_review.png}{Writing a post-contract bilateral rating and review}{fig:wf_rev_write}

\wffig[0.85]{images/community/m11_06_respond_to_review.png}{Publishing a response to a received contract review}{fig:wf_rev_reply}

Fosters community engagement and real-time collaboration while safeguarding content quality through automated AI toxicity scoring, administrative moderation queues, and soft-delete recovery.

Members explore topic categories (Figure~\ref{fig:wf_forum_list}) and publish rich-text forum posts screened automatically for policy violations (Figures~\ref{fig:wf_forum_post} and \ref{fig:wf_ai_mod}).

Participants coordinate project specifications via authenticated 1-on-1 Socket.IO conversations supporting attachments, read receipts, and message recall (Figures~\ref{fig:wf_chat_list} and \ref{fig:wf_chat_send}).
