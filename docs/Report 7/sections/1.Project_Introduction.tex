\section{Product Background}
The global knowledge economy has witnessed a structural shift toward remote freelance collaboration. In software engineering, graphic design, content creation, and digital marketing, organizations increasingly rely on agile on-demand talent rather than long-term physical employment. However, conventional online freelance marketplaces suffer from four persistent structural dysfunctions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2cm]
    \item \textbf{Identity Asymmetry and Credential Fraud:} Clients cannot reliably verify whether a freelancer's profile represents a real, qualified professional or an anonymous entity reusing stolen portfolios.
    \item \textbf{Financial Insecurity and Payment Default:} Freelancers frequently face non-payment, scope creep, or arbitrary cancellations after investing significant effort, while clients risk paying upfront for incomplete or subpar deliverables.
    \item \textbf{Ambiguous Contractual Governance:} Most agreements remain informal text chats lacking enforceable milestone terms, legal exportability, or clear digital acceptance criteria.
    \item \textbf{Biased and Prolonged Dispute Resolution:} Traditional platforms utilize centralized customer support agents who lack domain expertise to objectively adjudicate technical programming or creative disputes.
\end{enumerate}

Frevia was conceived to eliminate this trust deficit. By unifying \textbf{AI-driven eKYC biometric verification}, \textbf{milestone-based Stripe escrow payments}, \textbf{digitally signed legally binding contracts}, and a \textbf{tripartite dispute arbitration model} involving certified independent Experts, Frevia establishes a secure, transparent ecosystem where parties can collaborate safely without prior mutual trust.

\subsection*{Detailed Limitations of Existing Platforms and Frevia Solutions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm]
    \item \textbf{Upwork Limitation:} Upwork's extensive global talent pool results in severe proposal spamming and rampant account renting where unverified workers execute contracts under someone else's credentials. \\
    \textit{Frevia Resolution:} Mandatory biometric face verification before bidding or receiving payouts, tied cryptographically to a unique national ID (CCCD), preventing account sharing.
    \item \textbf{Fiverr Limitation:} Fixed gig models cannot accommodate complex, iterative, multi-stage engineering projects that require flexible milestone deliverables and custom specifications. \\
    \textit{Frevia Resolution:} Dynamic milestone creation and submission workflows with dedicated revision requests and partial escrow release capabilities.
    \item \textbf{vLance Limitation:} Lacks automated escrow infrastructure and relies on manual bank transfers, leading to administrative bottlenecks, delayed releases, and vulnerable transaction handling. \\
    \textit{Frevia Resolution:} Full integration with Stripe Connect Express and Custom Accounts, guaranteeing programmatic escrow capture and immediate payout upon milestone approval.
\end{itemize}

\section{Business Opportunity}
The Southeast Asian digital economy is expanding at over 20\% annually, with Vietnam emerging as a premier software outsourcing and creative hub. Over 2 million professionals engage in freelance activities domestically. However, the market suffers from a fragmented payment infrastructure and high fraud rates in informal social media deals (Facebook groups, Telegram). 

Frevia addresses this massive business opportunity by serving as the \textbf{trusted, localized intermediary}. By combining low platform commission rates (5--8\% compared to Upwork's 10--20\%), automated Stripe escrow, and localized eKYC compliant with Vietnamese ID formats, Frevia captures substantial market share among tech startups, digital agencies, and independent knowledge workers.

\section{Project Scope \& Limitations}

\subsection{Major Features (Scope)}
The Frevia platform delivers 21 major feature groups organized across core modules:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm]
    \item \textbf{FE-01: Authentication \& Session Management:} Multi-role credential registration, Google OAuth 2.0, OTP email verification, JWT issuance, device tracking, and multi-session revocation.
    \item \textbf{FE-02: Multi-Role Membership \& Profile Management:} Dynamic switching between Client, Freelancer, and Expert roles within a single account, with customized profile fields and portfolio displays.
    \item \textbf{FE-03: Identity Verification (eKYC):} Citizen ID (CCCD) upload, optical character recognition (OCR), 3D facial mesh liveness detection, and admin verification dashboard.
    \item \textbf{FE-04: Job Posting \& Browsing:} Advanced faceted search, skill tagging, fixed-budget estimation, deadline enforcement, draft/publish lifecycle, and bookmarking.
    \item \textbf{FE-05: Proposal \& Bidding Management:} Proposal submission with milestone cost breakdowns, cover letters, file attachments, withdrawal, interview invitations, and acceptance.
    \item \textbf{FE-06: Contract Lifecycle Management:} Offer generation, bilateral digital signatures, milestone schedules, contract amendment, and tamper-evident PDF generation.
    \item \textbf{FE-07: Milestone Delivery \& Progress Tracking:} Multi-stage milestone submission, review workflows, file attachments, revision change requests, and completion approvals.
    \item \textbf{FE-08: Escrow Payment Processing via Stripe:} Client milestone funding (PaymentIntent hold), automated escrow release upon approval, payout transfers, and saved payment methods.
    \item \textbf{FE-09: Dispute Arbitration:} Structured dispute filing, evidence submission, position statements, and expert arbitrator binding decisions.
    \item \textbf{FE-10: Real-time Communication \& Direct Messaging:} WebSocket-powered 1-on-1 conversations, unread counters, message recall, attachments, and typing indicators.
    \item \textbf{FE-11: Community Forum \& Knowledge Hub:} Category-based discussions, post authoring, moderation workflows, upvoting, commenting, and content reporting.
    \item \textbf{FE-12: System Administration \& RBAC:} Comprehensive permission matrix management, catalog maintenance (skills, categories, banners), audit logging, and trash bin restoration.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Limitations \& Exclusions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm]
    \item \textbf{LI-01 (Payment Gateway):} Payment and payout operations are provided exclusively through Stripe. Direct local domestic e-wallets (MoMo, ZaloPay) and cryptocurrency settlements are excluded in the current release.
    \item \textbf{LI-02 (Country Availability):} Stripe Connect onboarding depends on Stripe-supported countries. Payouts to countries unsupported by Stripe require manual intermediary processing.
    \item \textbf{LI-03 (Contract Pricing Model):} Only fixed-price milestone contracts are supported. Hourly contracts with background screenshot desktop time-tracking are excluded.
    \item \textbf{LI-04 (Native Mobile Client):} Frevia is delivered as a fully responsive Web Progressive Web Application (PWA); native iOS and Android binary applications are out of scope.
\end{itemize}
