The Freelance Marketplace Platform project adopts the Scrum framework as its primary management approach. Scrum provides an iterative and incremental development process that enables the team to continuously deliver valuable features while adapting to evolving project requirements. This approach promotes collaboration, transparency, and continuous improvement throughout the software development lifecycle.

The project team consists of members responsible for system analysis, UI/UX design, frontend development, backend development, testing, and project management. Through regular communication and collaboration, the team works together to ensure that project objectives are achieved efficiently and that the platform meets stakeholder and user expectations.

The platform aims to connect clients and freelancers through a secure digital marketplace where clients can post projects, hire suitable freelancers, and manage transactions through a Stripe-based payment mechanism. Stripe PaymentIntents, webhooks, refunds, and transfers are used to support milestone payments and payment-status tracking. To support this vision, project requirements are organized into a Product Backlog and prioritized according to business value and development priorities.

The development process is divided into five sprints over a three-month period. Each sprint focuses on delivering incremental improvements and essential functionalities, allowing the team to gather feedback, evaluate progress, and continuously refine the product. Core features include user management, project posting, proposal submission, contract management, communication tools, secure payment processing, and administrative functions.

By following Scrum principles, the project maintains flexibility, encourages frequent stakeholder feedback, and ensures the continuous delivery of a reliable and user-centered Freelance Marketplace Platform.
