The \textbf{Frevia} project (project code SEP490\_04) develops a web-based freelance marketplace that connects Clients and Freelancers in software, design, content, and consulting work. A Client publishes a job with a fixed-price budget, a deadline, required skills, categories, and the number of positions to fill; Freelancers submit proposals, either as drafts or as submitted applications with attachments and an expiry date; the Client compares proposals and hires a candidate, which generates a contract that both parties must sign. The contract carries a total amount and a fixed platform fee recorded on the contract, and the work is divided into milestones that are funded into escrow, submitted with files and links, optionally returned for changes with a due date, and, after approval, released as a Stripe payout. Completed contracts can be reviewed with an overall rating and a category breakdown, and each review can receive a response.

Beyond the core transaction, the platform provides electronic identity verification with an AI document and liveness pipeline, an administrative review queue for identity documents and profile revisions, a milestone-level dispute procedure with evidence attachments, response deadlines, and an expert arbitration decision, one-to-one real-time messaging over an authenticated WebSocket, a persistent notification record, a community forum with categories, comments, likes, reporting, and AI-assisted moderation, saved searches, job alerts, and job bookmarks, explainable advisory AI CV/job matching, an expert directory, advertisement banners, and a data-driven permission model that lets an administrator change what each role may do without a redeployment. Artificial intelligence is applied purely in an advisory capacity (curriculum vitae skill extraction, identity verification, forum content moderation, and section-aware job--candidate recommendation scoring); autonomous hiring or contract execution is outside the scope.

Based on the established backlog of \textbf{219 SMAR items} distributed across five development sprints (20/09/2026 -- 27/11/2026), the total estimated effort is \textbf{370 man-days}, as detailed in Table \ref{tab:scope-estimation} (\textit{Scope and Estimation}).

\subsubsection{Key Performance Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Timeliness (\%):} Deliver all sprints and milestones on schedule.\\
    \textit{Target:} 95\% of the tasks (i.e., 208 out of 219) must be completed within the sprint in which they are planned. Delayed tasks shall not exceed 5\% of the total.
    \item \textbf{Allocated Effort (man-days):} Optimise development resources, aligning effort with task complexity, rated low, medium, or high.\\
    \textit{Target:} Actual total effort shall not exceed 370 $\pm$ 5\% (max. 389 man-days). High-complexity tasks (the identity verification pipeline, Stripe escrow funding and webhooks, dispute arbitration with expert decision, and the real-time messaging gateway) are assigned to experienced team members and scheduled early in their sprint.
    \item \textbf{Defect Distribution:} Maintain high software quality throughout development, testing, and acceptance.\\
    \textit{Target:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item No critical defects remain open at the acceptance milestone.
        \item 95\% of high-priority defects fixed within 48 hours of detection.
        \item Medium-priority defects not exceeding 5\% of the total system defects.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
