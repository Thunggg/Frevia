\normalsize

The increments are cumulative. Increment 2 assumes that contracts and jobs from Increment 1 already exist, Increment 3 assumes that contracts and milestones already exist to support disputes and arbitration, Increment 4 provides complete financial settlement for milestones via Stripe escrow, and Increment 5 assumes that the freelancer profile and the verification record already exist. Document deliverables are produced once and then revised at each iteration instead of being rewritten, which is why the SRS and the SDS appear four times in the table.
