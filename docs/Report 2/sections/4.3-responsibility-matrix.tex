Every row has exactly one member who reviews the work: that member confirms the output before it is demonstrated at the Sprint Review and reports its status. Where a row lists several members who do the work, the work is split by module rather than duplicated, and the split is recorded on the task board before the sprint begins. The rows that carry the highest technical risk -- Stripe escrow and reconciliation, dispute arbitration, and AI integration -- pair the member who does the work with at least one supporter so that a second person can check the implementation.
