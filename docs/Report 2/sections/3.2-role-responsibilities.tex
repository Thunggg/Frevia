\textbf{Role Name} & \textbf{Description} \\
\hline

Product Owner &
The visionary leader responsible for the product's direction and backlog prioritization, ensuring the product meets customer needs.
\\ \hline

Scrum Master &
The facilitator and coach, ensuring the Scrum process runs smoothly and the team follows Agile practices.
\\ \hline

UX/UI Designer &
Combines user research with visual design to create interfaces that are intuitive, user-friendly, and visually appealing.
\\ \hline

Software Architect &
Technical visionary responsible for defining the system architecture, technology stack, and development standards.
\\ \hline

Frontend Developer &
Develops responsive and interactive user interfaces, translating design mockups into functional web pages and ensuring a seamless user experience.
\\ \hline

Backend Developer &
Implements server-side logic, APIs, database management, authentication, and business processes that support application functionality.
\\ \hline

AI Developer &
Designs, develops, and trains intelligent models and algorithms to provide AI-powered features and automated decision-making capabilities.
\\ \hline

Payment Integration Developer &
Develops and maintains Stripe Checkout or PaymentIntent flows, webhook handling, payment status synchronization, refunds, transfers, and transaction reconciliation.
\\ \hline

Requirement Analyst &
Acts as the bridge between stakeholders and the development team by gathering, analyzing, documenting, and validating project requirements.
\\ \hline

Functional Tester &
Ensures that all system features and functions operate according to the specified requirements through systematic testing and defect verification.
\\ \hline

Non-Functional Tester &
Evaluates system quality attributes such as performance, scalability, security, reliability, and usability to ensure the application meets quality standards.
\\ \hline
