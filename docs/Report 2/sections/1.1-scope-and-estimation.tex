The work breakdown below is the basis for sprint planning. Each row is a SMAR (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant) item that can be demonstrated at the end of the sprint in which it is scheduled, and the identifier in the second column is the reference used by the task board, the review checklist, and the test cases. Complexity is rated Low, Medium or High according to technical uncertainty and integration risk rather than to effort alone; effort is estimated in man-days and rounded to whole days. Module header rows carry no estimate because their effort is already distributed across the items beneath them.

All five three-week sprints maintain a balanced effort envelope (71--75 man-days) aligned with the five-member team's capacity per sprint. Sprint 1 delivers project setup, authentication, role-based access foundations, profiles, and the job marketplace. Sprint 2 covers skill and job catalogues, bookmarks, saved searches, proposals, hiring decisions, contracts, and eKYC capture. Sprint 3 delivers milestones, file deliverables, submissions, real-time messaging, notifications, and platform administration including identity review. Sprint 4 focuses on dispute management with expert arbitration, Stripe escrow payments, community forum, and advertisement banners. Sprint 5 delivers reviews and ratings, curriculum vitae handling with AI analysis, expert directory, social features, localisation, dashboards, quality hardening, and acceptance testing.
