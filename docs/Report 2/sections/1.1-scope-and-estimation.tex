The four two-week sprints maintain a balanced effort envelope aligned with the team's velocity after the foundational first sprint. Sprint 1 carries project setup, identity verification, job marketplace, and contract management. Sprint 2 focuses on proposals, hiring decisions, and milestone lifecycle with file deliverables and submissions. Sprint 3 delivers real-time messaging, notifications, platform administration, and formal dispute management with expert escalation. Sprint 4 delivers full Stripe Connect onboarding, escrow funding, hold deposits, release transfers, and webhook reconciliation. Sprint 5 deliberately reserves effort for quality hardening, regression testing, and user acceptance.
