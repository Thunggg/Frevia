Complexity is rated Low, Medium or High according to technical uncertainty and integration risk rather than to effort alone; effort is estimated in man-days and rounded to whole days. Module header rows carry no estimate because their effort is already distributed across the items beneath them.

\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
All five development sprints (20/09/2026 -- 27/11/2026) maintain a bounded effort envelope (59--92 man-days across the five-member team). Sprint 1 delivers project setup, authentication, role-based access foundations, profiles, the job marketplace, and contract management. Sprint 2 covers skill and job catalogues, bookmarks, saved searches, proposals, hiring decisions, eKYC capture, and milestones (including files and submissions). Sprint 3 delivers real-time messaging, notifications, platform administration including identity review, and dispute management with evidence uploads and expert arbitration. Sprint 4 focuses on Stripe escrow payments, community forum, advertisement banners, and trust indicators. Sprint 5 delivers reviews and ratings, curriculum vitae handling with AI analysis, expert directory, social features, localisation, dashboards, quality hardening, and acceptance testing.
